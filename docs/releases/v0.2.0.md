SEO Evidence Lab 0.2.0 connects saved news and research exports to a versioned review workflow. Everything runs locally from saved files.

- **Bring in saved news.** Import saved news-agent exports with their source URL, publication and observation times, content hash and capture details; successive versions and failed attempts are kept for review in a private inbox.
- **Review proposed changes before they count.** Each impact proposal is tied to its source and stays inactive and unapproved until it is reviewed.
- **Replay and search more.** More replay criteria beyond headings, a larger retrieval benchmark, and read-only MCP access to pinned local datasets with content hashes.

**Assets:** `seo-evidence-lab-v0.2.0-source.zip` is the runnable source release and `SHA256SUMS` lets you check it. Saved-import previews, fixes and test counts are in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/j-zulick/seo-evidence-lab/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#v020).

## Boundaries
- The retrieval benchmark is small, synthetic and agent-labeled; independent labels are pending. There are no live Ahrefs, Search Console, CrUX, Reddit, Jev or Langfuse runs, and observational data does not show causation.
- Not for shared hosting: multi-account identity and route scoping come later. Hosting stays paused.
