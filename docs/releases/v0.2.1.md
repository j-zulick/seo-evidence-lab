# v0.2.1

Groundwork release: approve reviewed guidance into versioned releases, keep accounts isolated, and prepare independent labeling and real-export intake. It all runs locally from saved files.

- **Approve a guidance change and see what it affects:** `python -m seo_qa.guidance_release approve|list|affected` turns a reviewed news proposal into an immutable, chained guidance version and replays audits under it.
- **Score retrieval with independent labels:** `python -m seo_qa.labeling sheet` writes a blank sheet; `score` compares baseline vs expansion and reports agreement.
- **Load your own search exports:** `python -m seo_qa.provider_intake init|run <folder>` normalizes saved GSC/CrUX/Ahrefs exports without touching the originals.

**Assets:** `seo-evidence-lab-v0.2.1-source.zip` is the source release and `SHA256SUMS` lets you check it. Full details, including multi-account isolation: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/j-zulick/seo-evidence-lab/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Boundaries
- No real news discovery has been reviewed and approved yet, and no independent labels or real exports have been processed.
- Multi-account isolation is tested offline only; not for shared hosting. Hosting stays paused.
