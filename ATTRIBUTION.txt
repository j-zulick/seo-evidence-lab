Project origins
Sweepr (https://sweepr-code.vercel.app) supplied the initial documentation-drift idea: verify documentation against a changing codebase through a GitHub/Notion workflow. Historical project records show that this concept evolved into SEO release verification.
Creator project description: https://alexbbritton.com/

Botworthy Index (https://botworthyindex.com/scoreboard) was a later reference for a separately implemented local content-quotability adaptation, identified in its methodology as public v3.0.4.

These references identify product and methodology influences. They do not establish copied implementation, verified current vendor behavior or endorsement. Repository licensing is an owner decision.
