How this score is calculated

Each applicable criterion has equal weight for each source. Score = 1 + 9 × passing criteria ÷ applicable criteria, rounded down to one decimal place. With no applicable criteria, the score is 1.0 and the assessment stays incomplete.

Unknown, blocked and missing evidence remain visible. A numerical score of 7 or above meets the score threshold; a completed, current assessment also requires resolved evidence and complete coverage. Human acceptance remains a separate review.
